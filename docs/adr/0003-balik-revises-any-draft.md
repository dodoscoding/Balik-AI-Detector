# Balik revises any draft, including AI drafts

We considered two promises. The first was narrow: a writer's own draft becomes clearer, stays in their voice, and is less likely to be falsely flagged. The second was broad: any draft, including an AI draft, reads as human writing and passes the provider detectors. We chose the broad promise. Development therefore centers on AI drafts and mixed drafts, with human-written text as the restraint check.

## Consequences

- The voice criterion applies only to drafts that have a writer. AI drafts are judged on blind preference, meaning, and restraint.
- Many universities treat submitting a revised AI draft as one's own coursework as misconduct, and Turnitin targets bypasser output. Friends who use Balik on coursework carry that risk.
- Providers may refuse paid benchmarking access for this use (see ADR 0002).
