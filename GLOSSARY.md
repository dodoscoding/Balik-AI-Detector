# Balik

Balik revises drafts so they read as natural human writing, and measures the result with AI-writing detectors. This glossary fixes the words used in the roadmap, issues, and tests.

## Drafts and sources

**Draft**:
A passage of text submitted to Balik for revision, whatever its origin.
_Avoid_: Essay, paper, ödev (when meaning the text itself)

**Brief**:
The draft's assignment, one-sentence writing goal, and requested tone, given identically to every editor.
_Avoid_: Prompt (reserved for model instructions), instructions

**Source type**:
Whether a draft is human-written, an AI draft, a mixed draft, or unknown-origin text.

**Human-written text**:
Text published or submitted before 30 November 2022, or a participating writer's own draft.
_Avoid_: Real text, original, authentic

**Unknown-origin text**:
Public text dated on or after 30 November 2022, which may be AI-assisted.

**AI draft**:
A draft generated by a model from the same prompt or source as a human-written document.
_Avoid_: Fake, synthetic essay

**Mixed draft**:
A draft that combines human-written and model-generated passages, with the share recorded.
_Avoid_: Hybrid

**Writer**:
The identifiable person who wrote a human-written or mixed draft. AI drafts have no writer.
_Avoid_: Author (except in corpus metadata), user

**Passage already meeting the brief**:
A passage the writer marked before editing as one that should stay unchanged.
_Avoid_: Polished passage, protected passage

## Corpus

**Development corpus**:
The public-archive documents, and the drafts derived from them, used to build and tune Balik.
_Avoid_: Training data (unless fine-tuning), dataset

**Archive document**:
One document taken from a public archive, with its source, license, and date recorded.

**Manifest**:
The committed record of every archive document and derived draft: source, license, date, register, source type, and split.

**Register**:
The kind of writing a document represents, such as school student writing, university student writing, or published academic writing.
_Avoid_: Genre, domain

**Writing context**:
Academic or general writing. Together with a language it forms a combination, the unit for beta decisions.

**Development set**:
Drafts Balik may be tuned and compared on.

**Test set**:
Drafts reserved before any comparison and opened only for beta qualification.
_Avoid_: Final set, holdout (alone), nihai küme

## Editors

**Balik edit**:
A revision produced by the current Balik revision approach.

**Basic copyedit**:
A revision from a fixed prompt asking the same model to correct grammar and improve clarity while preserving meaning and voice.
_Avoid_: Baseline (alone), copy edit

**Naive humanizer**:
A revision from a fixed prompt asking the same model to rewrite the draft so it sounds human-written.

**Revision approach**:
The prompts, model, settings, and any examples that together produce a Balik edit.

## Detection

**Local detector set**:
The open-source detectors run on the reference machine during development. Its scores are never presented as provider results.
_Avoid_: The detector, AI score

**Provider detector**:
A commercial AI-writing detector named in the product target: Turnitin or GPTZero.

**Not measured**:
The recorded status of a provider result when access, permission, or language support is missing.

## Evaluation

**Fidelity check**:
The automated comparison of a revision with its source for changed numbers, citations, quotations, named entities, and hedges.
_Avoid_: Meaning review (that is the human check)

**Meaning review**:
A person's review of a Balik edit against its source for any changed claim, attribution, quantity, quotation, citation, or degree of certainty.

**Restraint**:
How little Balik changes human-written text and passages already meeting the brief.

**Friends gate**:
The blind comparison with friends that decides whether to build the editor. It is a rehearsal, not beta evidence.
_Avoid_: M1 rehearsal, prova (when meaning the gate)

**Beta qualification**:
The funded evaluation that applies the full beta criteria and provider measurements to each combination.

**Reference machine**:
The Mac on which every reported comparison runs with a pinned runtime.
